\textbf{Common simulation baseline.} All position-control comparisons use the original course signals $q_d=[U_v,U_h]^\mathsf{T}$ over 0--60 s, sampled at 0.002 s with the supplied scaling and linear interpolation. They combine smoothed yaw/pitch transitions ($T_r=2.5$ s) and the original slow pitch sinusoid. Initial states are zero. The supplied quantization/noise setting is used for the complete-cascade baseline; ideal measurements isolate motor-bandwidth effects. Motor-speed, acceleration, settling-time and disturbance tests are supplementary diagnostics.
